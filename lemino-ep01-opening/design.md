# Visual direction
Concept: an expedition into the negative space between sixteen known giants and an imagined seventeenth.
Palette: charcoal #151d1f, ivory #eee6d3, sandstone #b6a183, mist turquoise #8ac2bb.
Typography: locally embedded Thai sans for narrative, Thai serif for main title, DejaVu Serif for monumental numerals.
Five full-frame schematic illustrations, original vector art. Not gameplay, not a faithful map or architectural reconstruction. Landscape contours and shrine silhouette are interpretive.
Focal points: shrine on horizon, sixteen numbered markers, garden on summit, missing seventeenth, final title.
Motion: layered camera translations and scale, SVG path draw, staggered markers. No bounce or fast glitch. Silence for visual first cut.
